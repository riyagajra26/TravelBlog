Beautiful Places in India

Explore some of the most amazing tourist destinations in India.

Jaipur, Rajasthan

Jaipur, also known as the Pink City, is famous for its beautiful palaces, forts and colourful markets.

Best Time: October to March

Duration: 3–4 Days

Famous Places: Hawa Mahal, Amber Fort

Kashmir

Kashmir is known for its snow-covered mountains, beautiful valleys, lakes and peaceful gardens.

Best Time: March to October

Duration: 5–6 Days

Famous Places: Dal Lake, Gulmarg

Kerala

Kerala, God's Own Country, is famous for its backwaters, green landscapes, beaches and houseboats.

Best Time: September to March

Duration: 4–5 Days

Famous Places: Munnar, Alleppey
